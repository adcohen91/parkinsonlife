Welcome

You Deserve Better Than a Dozen Disconnected Websites

If you or someone you love has been diagnosed with Parkinson's disease, you already know the experience of searching for help online: a medical journal here, an outdated PDF there, a forum post from 2014, a charity website with broken links. Hours of searching. A handful of useful results. And never quite the full picture you need.

ParkinsonLife was built to change that. Not as another link directory or a newsletter archive — but as a living, professionally maintained platform that grows with the community it serves, and treats every visitor with the intelligence and respect they deserve.

The Parkinson's community doesn't need more scattered information. It needs a single, trusted home where knowledge, tools, and support come together — and where the standard never slips.

We are that home. And we are just getting started.

The Mission

Why ParkinsonLife — and Why It's Different

There are excellent organizations doing important work in the Parkinson's space. We respect all of them. But a platform designed to genuinely serve the day-to-day needs of patients, caregivers, and families — one that connects the dots across every domain of Parkinson's life — has been missing. Until now.

ParkinsonLife is built on a simple belief: that managing Parkinson's well requires access to expertise across many disciplines, not just one. Neurology is foundational — but it doesn't exist in isolation. Nutrition affects medication efficacy. Exercise influences motor control. Sleep quality shapes cognitive function. Mental health underpins everything. Caregiving is its own discipline. And navigating the healthcare system requires information that no single specialist typically provides.

ParkinsonLife pulls all of it together.

Applications in Development

Tools Being Built Right Now

ParkinsonLife isn't just a content platform — it is becoming a suite of practical applications built specifically for the needs of Parkinson's patients. Here is what is currently under active development:

🥗
Live · Parkinson's Nutrition

Parkinson's Diet Application

Nutrition is one of the most under-discussed and impactful factors in Parkinson's management. This application provides personalized dietary guidance specifically tailored to Parkinson's patients — accounting for levodopa timing, protein interaction, nutrient deficiencies common in Parkinson's, and day-to-day quality-of-life goals. Finally, nutrition advice that understands your condition.

● Live Now
🎙️
In Development · Voice & Speech

Parky — Voice Therapy Application

Speech and swallowing challenges affect a significant portion of the Parkinson's community, yet access to consistent, professional speech therapy is difficult and expensive for many. Parky is a voice therapy application designed to bring structured speech exercises, vocal strength training, and communication confidence tools directly to patients — from the comfort and privacy of home, on their schedule.

🔨 In Development
💊
In Development · Medication Management

Prescription Scheduler

Medication timing is not just important for Parkinson's patients — it is everything. The difference between taking levodopa 30 minutes early or late can mean hours of dramatically reduced function. This smart scheduling tool is being built to help patients and caregivers manage complex, multi-drug medication routines with precision, reminders, and peace of mind — because no one should have to hold this in their head alone.

🔨 In Development
🚀

More applications are in planning. The roadmap includes tools for exercise tracking calibrated to Parkinson's motor patterns, sleep quality monitoring, symptom journaling with trend analysis, and care coordination tools for families managing care across multiple providers. ParkinsonLife is being built to grow with you.

From the Founder

Built From the Inside Out

ParkinsonLife wasn't built by a team that researched Parkinson's and decided to build a website about it. It was built by someone who lives with it every day.

I was diagnosed with Parkinson's Dysautonomia. I know what it feels like to search for answers at 2am. I know the frustration of getting different information from different doctors. I know how isolating it can be to try to piece together a picture of your own condition from fragments scattered across the internet.

I also know what it is like to keep building — to take breaks, to push through, to refuse to let a diagnosis define what is possible. Over the past few days alone, what has been built here is something I am genuinely, deeply proud of. And I am building more as you read this.

— ParkinsonLife was built for you, because I am you.

The platform is powered by ADC Risk Horizon, which volunteers 100% of its engineering, infrastructure, and development expertise to ParkinsonLife. Every technical decision — from security and global reliability to design and application architecture — is made to the same professional standard we apply in enterprise engagements. Because the Parkinson's community deserves nothing less.
